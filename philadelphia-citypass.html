The maths

When the pass wins

◆

Expensive sights

Add the National Constitution Center at $24.95 and a four-attraction list reaches $91.95 against the $84 pass: about $7.95 saved.

◆

Cheap lists

At the five-attraction tier ($98), the pass needs five paid sights averaging about $19.60. Four verified sights average $22.99, so a fifth comparable sight pushes the pass ahead.

Questions

Philadelphia CityPASS questions

Is the three-attraction pass worth it?

For Franklin, the American Revolution Museum and Eastern State, the gate total is about $67 against $66 — effectively break-even. Convenience may still justify it.

Can I choose different attractions?

Yes, any three, four or five from the list of nine, in any order within nine days.

Are child prices lower?

Yes: $54 for C3, $66 for C4 and $78 for C5, checked November 2026.
