# City Pass Index

Independent comparison of city tourist passes: verified pass prices, attraction gate prices, break-even maths and a calculator. No pay-to-play rankings.

## What this site answers
- Is a city pass worth it? Pick attractions, compare gate prices, find the break-even.
- Which pass: CityPASS, Go City Explorer, Go City All-Inclusive, New York Pass.
- What do attractions actually cost at the gate? Download the CSV.
