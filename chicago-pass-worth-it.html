Chicago

Is Chicago CityPASS worth it?

Three realistic lists, the gate prices we have verified and the break-even line. The pass is not automatically the answer.

3 scenarios$144Checked November 2026
Scenario 1

A short list

360 Chicago ($35) and Adler Planetarium ($25) total $60. The other three attractions need to average more than $28 for the $144 pass to beat buying direct.

Scenario 2

A fuller trip

A first trip with Shedd, Skydeck, the architecture cruise, the Field Museum and 360 Chicago is exactly the shape the pass is built for; the gate total usually clears $144.

◆

The threshold

At $144 for 5 of 8, each attraction needs to be worth about $28.80 at the gate.

Scenario 3

When buying direct wins

A museum-only visit (Art Institute, Field, Adler) can fall below the pass price; those three at the gate are unlikely to reach $144.

Questions

Worth-it questions

Is Chicago CityPASS worth it?

It depends on the list. For the fuller trip above, usually yes; for a short museum-only visit, buying direct can be cheaper.

How many attractions do I need?

The pass covers 5 of 8; you cannot buy fewer. Compare the pass price with the gate prices of the attractions you would actually visit.

Are the prices current?

Pass prices and verified gate prices were checked in November 2026; dynamic pricing can change the checkout price.
