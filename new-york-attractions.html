How to use it

Build the five before you buy

Sort the table by what you would actually pay, not by what sounds essential. Three observation decks are similar experiences; one deck, one museum, one ferry, one neighbourhood walk and one cruise is usually a better trip — and often a cheaper list.

◆

The break-even

CityPASS costs about $151 for five attractions, so the average visit needs to be worth about $30. If your five average below that, buying direct wins.

Questions

Attraction questions

Are these the final prices?

They are the adult “from” prices checked in November 2026. Dynamic pricing can make the checkout price higher, especially at popular sunset slots.

Which attraction is the best value?

The observation decks ($42–46) and the 9/11 Museum are the strongest value for pass maths; the free sights are free either way.

Does CityPASS include the Empire State 102nd floor?

No, only the main observation deck. The 102nd floor is a paid add-on.
