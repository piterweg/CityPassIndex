Count the gate prices first

Write down the paid attractions you would actually visit, add their gate prices, then compare with the pass. Do not buy the pass first.

Questions

Go City vs CityPASS questions

Which is cheaper?

CityPASS is cheaper per attraction for five expensive sights. Go City Explorer is cheaper if you only want two or three.

Can I combine them?

No — that double-pays. Pick one pass or buy the remaining sights directly.

Are the prices final?

No. Both operators change prices and inclusions; figures here are the adult prices checked in November 2026.
