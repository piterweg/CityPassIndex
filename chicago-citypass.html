The maths

When the pass wins

◆

Expensive sights

A first trip with Shedd, Skydeck, the architecture cruise, the Field Museum and 360 Chicago is exactly the shape the pass is built for; the gate total usually clears $144.

◆

Cheap lists

A museum-only visit (Art Institute, Field, Adler) can fall below the pass price; those three at the gate are unlikely to reach $144.

Questions

Chicago CityPASS questions

Is Chicago CityPASS worth it?

For five sights including Shedd and Skydeck, usually yes. For three museums only, the gate total may stay under $144.

How many attractions does it include?

Five from eight; two are fixed (Shedd and Skydeck) and you choose three more.

Is there a smaller option?

Yes, Chicago C3 covers three attractions for $109.
