Boston

Is Boston CityPASS worth it?

Three realistic lists, the gate prices we have verified and the break-even line. The pass is not automatically the answer.

3 scenarios$84Checked November 2026
Scenario 1

A short list

New England Aquarium starts at $34.95. The other three attractions need to average more than $16.35 for the $84 pass to beat buying direct.

Scenario 2

A fuller trip

A typical first trip pairs the aquarium with the Museum of Science, the harbour cruise and View Boston. If those three average $25–$32 at the gate, the pass wins by a clear margin.

◆

The threshold

At $84 for 4 of 7, each attraction needs to be worth about $21 at the gate.

Scenario 3

When buying direct wins

A museum-only trip (Harvard Museum of Natural History, MFA, a small museum and one more) can fall below $84; check the gate prices before committing.

Questions

Worth-it questions

Is Boston CityPASS worth it?

It depends on the list. For the fuller trip above, usually yes; for a short museum-only visit, buying direct can be cheaper.

How many attractions do I need?

The pass covers 4 of 7; you cannot buy fewer. Compare the pass price with the gate prices of the attractions you would actually visit.

Are the prices current?

Pass prices and verified gate prices were checked in November 2026; dynamic pricing can change the checkout price.
