About

Passes, prices, no theatre

City Pass Index is an independent data project: checked prices, break-even maths and a calculator that does not ask for your email.

IndependentDated dataNo pay-to-play

Why this exists

Pass marketing is built to make the maths feel obvious. It usually is not: the value depends entirely on which five attractions you would visit anyway. This site exists to put the gate prices next to the pass price and let the numbers decide.

Funding

At launch the site is free to read and carries no affiliate links. If that changes, affected pages will disclose it before the link and the ranking will not be for sale.

Corrections

Prices change. If a figure is wrong, tell us the page, the operator's current price and the date you checked it; we will update the page and the checked date.
