How to choose

Match the pass to the trip

The cheapest pass is the one you will actually use.

Short trip

The warning

Do not buy a pass first

◆

List the sights before the pass

Write down the paid attractions you would actually visit, add their gate prices, then compare. A pass is a discount on volume — if you would not buy the volume, it is not a discount.

Questions

Pass questions

Which pass is cheapest?

None is cheapest in general. CityPASS wins for five expensive sights; Explorer wins for three or four; all-inclusive passes only win with very high daily usage.

Are these prices final?

No. Pass prices and inclusions change; they are the operator's adult prices checked in November 2026. Confirm at checkout.

Do you earn commission?

Not at launch. If that changes, the page will say so before the link.
