Go City New York, reviewed

Two different products share the Go City name: Explorer, a pack of attraction choices, and All-Inclusive, a day pass. They solve different trips.

The maths

Explorer vs CityPASS

◆

Explorer wins for three sights

CityPASS is a fixed five. If you only want a deck, a museum and a cruise, Explorer's two- or three-choice tiers can be cheaper.

◆

CityPASS wins for five

For five expensive sights, the $149 CityPASS usually beats a five-choice Explorer pack. Do the arithmetic on your actual list.

Practical

What to check before buying

  • 01

    Not every attraction is included

    Go City works with a long list of partners, but premium experiences and some observation decks carry restrictions or need reservations.

  • 02

    Reservations still apply

    A pass is not a queue jump. Popular attractions use timed entry and can sell out on weekends.

  • 03

    Unused choices expire

    Explorer choices must be used within the validity window; do not buy a ten-choice pack for a three-sight trip.

  • 04

    All-Inclusive needs a busy day

    At $169 for one day, the day pass only makes sense if you would genuinely pay for several sights in that day.

Questions

Go City questions

Is Go City better than CityPASS?

Neither is universally better. Explorer suits three or four sights; CityPASS suits five expensive ones. The right answer depends on your list.

Can I use both?

You can buy both, but you would be double-paying for any attraction covered twice. Pick one and buy the rest direct.

Are prices fixed?

No. Both products change prices and inclusions; the figures here are adult prices checked in November 2026.
