The threshold

At $144 for five attractions, each visit needs to be worth about $28.80 at the gate. Shedd and Skydeck alone are typically close to that, so the pass is easier to justify than a museum-only bundle.

Gate prices for individual attractions are still being checked for this city. The pass price and inclusions above are verified; we publish a full gate-price index only when every figure has a source and a date.
