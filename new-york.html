Questions

New York pass questions

Is CityPASS New York worth it?

It depends on your five. At about $30.20 per attraction, the pass wins when you would visit five expensive sights such as the Empire State, Top of the Rock, the 9/11 Museum and the Statue ferry.

Are the Met and MoMA included?

MoMA is one of the ten options; the Met is not. Check the current list before you buy.

How long is the pass valid?

Nine days from first use, with a $2 processing fee at checkout.
