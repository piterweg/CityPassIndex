Questions

Attraction questions

Why are some prices missing?

We only publish gate prices we have checked against the operator. Several attractions use dynamic pricing or block automated checks; those stay “in research” until verified.

Does the pass include add-ons?

No. Premium add-ons and special exhibitions are usually separate; the pass covers standard admission as described by the operator.

Are child prices different?

Yes. Child passes are cheaper; check the operator's current child price before relying on the adult break-even.
