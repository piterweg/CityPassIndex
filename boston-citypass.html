The maths

When the pass wins

◆

Expensive sights

A typical first trip pairs the aquarium with the Museum of Science, the harbour cruise and View Boston. If those three average $25–$32 at the gate, the pass wins by a clear margin.

◆

Cheap lists

A museum-only trip (Harvard Museum of Natural History, MFA, a small museum and one more) can fall below $84; check the gate prices before committing.

Questions

Boston CityPASS questions

Is Boston CityPASS worth it?

Usually for a first trip with the aquarium, a museum, the harbour cruise and an observation deck. For three cheap museums, buying direct can win.

How many attractions does it include?

Four from a list of seven; you choose which four.

Are reservations needed?

Some attractions require timed entry, especially the aquarium and cruises.
