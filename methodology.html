Methodology

How the numbers are built

A price without a date and a source is a rumour. Every figure on this site has both.

Official sourcesDated checksNo pay-to-play

Gate prices

Attraction prices come from the operator's own booking page, not a reseller. We record the lowest adult "from" price on the checked date and note when dynamic pricing means the checkout price can be higher.

Pass prices

Pass prices and inclusions come from the pass operator's official sales page. The break-even is the pass price divided by the number of attractions it covers; that is the average gate value each visit must beat.

What is excluded

Transport, food, add-ons (crown access, the 102nd floor, City Climb and similar) and packages. Those belong in the attraction guides, not in the pass comparison.

Updates

Prices are re-checked quarterly and whenever an operator announces a change. The checked date on each page is the date of the latest sweep.

◆

Corrections welcome

If a price has changed, tell us and we will fix it with a date. The site is independent and no operator pays for placement.
