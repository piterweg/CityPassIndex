The maths

What the break-even means

◆

The threshold

The three-attraction pass costs $66, so each visit needs to be worth about $22 at the gate. The five-attraction pass costs $98, or about $19.60 per visit — a lower bar per attraction, but only if you would visit five.

Gate prices for individual attractions are still being checked for this city. The pass price and inclusions above are verified; we publish a full gate-price index only when every figure has a source and a date.
