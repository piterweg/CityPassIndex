How to read it

The pass wins when your list is expensive

◆

Check the gate prices first

Write down the paid sights you would genuinely visit. If their gate total is below the pass price, buying direct wins — and the calculator says so.

◆

What it does not include

Transport, food and paid add-ons such as the 102nd floor, crown access or City Climb. Prices are gate rates, not package prices.

Questions

Calculator questions

Why do I have to enter prices for some cities?

Because we only publish gate prices we have checked ourselves. New York is fully checked; for Philadelphia, Boston and Chicago the pass price is verified, and you can type the gate price you see on the official ticket page to get the full saving.

Does the calculator include children?

No. It uses adult prices and a number of adults. Child passes are cheaper and the break-even is usually lower, so do not reuse the adult result for a family with children.

What if I want more attractions than the pass covers?

Then the pass covers its limit and you pay the gate price for the extras. Add those extras separately to compare honestly.

When are the prices checked?

Pass prices and inclusions were checked in November 2026. Gate prices can change with dynamic pricing, especially at weekends and sunset slots.
