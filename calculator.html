How to read it

The pass wins when your list is expensive

◆

The break-even

CityPASS costs about $151 for five attractions, so the average visit needs to be worth about $30 at the gate. If your five cost more than that, the pass saves money.

◆

What the calculator does not include

Transport, food and add-ons such as the 102nd floor, crown access or City Climb. Prices are gate rates, not package prices.

Questions

Calculator questions

Why five attractions?

New York CityPASS covers five attractions from ten. The pass is sold as one price, so the maths is about which five you would choose.

Are the prices exact?

They are the adult 'from' prices checked in November 2026. Dynamic pricing can make the gate price higher at weekends and sunset slots.

What if I want six attractions?

Then the pass covers five and you pay the gate price for the sixth. Add that separately to compare honestly.
